# Campus Academic Policies & Regulations
**Division of Academic Affairs | Office of the University Registrar**
**Effective for Academic Year 2026-2027**

---

## 1. Grading Scale and Standards
All undergraduate and graduate courses adhere to the standardized university grading framework:

| Letter Grade | Percentage Range | Grade Point (GPA) | Performance Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 93.0% – 100% | 4.0 | Excellent |
| A- | 90.0% – 92.9% | 3.7 | Superior |
| B+ | 87.0% – 89.9% | 3.3 | Very Good |
| B | 83.0% – 86.9% | 3.0 | Good (Prerequisite threshold for core majors) |
| B- | 80.0% – 82.9% | 2.7 | Satisfactory |
| C+ | 77.0% – 79.9% | 2.3 | Marginal Pass |
| C | 73.0% – 76.9% | 2.0 | Minimum Passing |
| C- to F | Below 73.0% | < 2.0 | Failing / Course Repeat Required |

## 2. Late Submission Policy
Maintaining course schedule integrity is essential for fair evaluation:
- **Standard Deductions:** Work submitted past the published deadline incurs a penalty of **10% per calendar day** (24-hour window), up to a maximum of **3 calendar days late (30% deduction)**.
- **Final Cutoff:** Assignments submitted more than 72 hours (3 days) after the deadline receive an automatic score of zero unless an approved emergency extension is granted.
- **Emergency Extensions:** In cases of documented medical illness or acute personal emergencies, students must submit a formal extension petition through the Student Portal at least **12 hours before** the assignment deadline. Retroactive excuses require Dean of Students authorization.
- **Final Projects & Capstones:** Due to university grade submission deadlines, final capstone submissions cannot be submitted late without explicit written approval from the Academic Dean.

## 3. Academic Integrity and AI Usage Guidelines
The university values intellectual honesty, rigorous inquiry, and ethical scholarship:
- **Authorized AI Usage:** AI tools (such as large language models, code completion assistants, and chat interfaces) are permitted only when explicitly authorized in the course syllabus or assignment instructions. When allowed, all AI-generated contributions must be explicitly documented in a citations section describing the prompt and model used.
- **Prohibited Conduct:** Submitting AI-generated code, essays, or solutions as one's own original work without explicit citation constitutes plagiarism. Unauthorized collaboration, contract cheating, or tampering with automated testing suites is strictly prohibited.
- **Sanctions:** Violations of academic integrity are investigated by the Academic Honor Board. First offenses result in a grade of zero on the assignment and a formal disciplinary letter. Repeated or egregious violations result in course failure (grade of XF) and possible suspension or expulsion.

## 4. Attendance and Participation
- **Mandatory Attendance:** In lecture and laboratory courses, students are expected to attend at least **80% of scheduled sessions** to receive course credit.
- **Excused Absences:** University-sanctioned athletic competitions, recognized religious observances, and documented illnesses are considered excused if submitted via the Student Portal within 5 business days of the absence.
- **Excessive Absences:** Unexcused absences exceeding 20% of class sessions may result in an administrative withdrawal (grade of W) or a failing grade (grade of FA).

## 5. Course Drop and Withdrawal Deadlines
- **Add/Drop Period:** Students may add or drop courses without academic record notation during the first **10 instructional days** of the semester.
- **Course Withdrawal:** Students may withdraw from a course with a transcript grade of "W" until the end of the **10th week of classes (April 2, 2027)**. After the 10th week, withdrawals are not permitted except under extraordinary certified medical circumstances.
