# Student Guide & Academic Support Services
**Division of Student Affairs | Center for Academic Excellence**
**Campus Edition 2026-2027**

---

## 1. Academic Advising and Mentorship
Every enrolled student is paired with a departmental faculty advisor and a professional academic advisor:
- **Primary Advisor Role:** Assists with semester course registration, degree audits, graduation pathway planning, and academic petitions.
- **Mandatory Advising:** First-year students and students on academic warning must meet with their academic advisor prior to registration for each subsequent term.
- **Advising Office Location:** Student Services Center, Suite 210.
- **Advising Hours:** Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM. Walk-ins are accepted on Wednesdays from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM; all other consultations require appointment booking via the Campus Portal.

## 2. Tutoring Center and Writing Lab
The Center for Academic Excellence provides free, peer-led and specialist academic tutoring:
- **STEM Learning Hub:** Located in Turing Hall, Room 104. Offers drop-in peer tutoring for computer science, mathematics, statistics, and physics. Open Monday through Thursday, 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM, and Sunday 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM.
- **University Writing Lab:** Assists students with research papers, technical reports, capstone documentation, and graduate school applications. One-on-one 45-minute consultation sessions can be scheduled online or in person at Library Annex 3B.
- **Virtual Tutoring:** Available 24/7 via the student dashboard for introductory programming (Python, Java, C++) and calculus.

## 3. Instructor and TA Office Hours
Faculty and teaching assistants hold weekly office hours to support student learning:
- **Dr. Elena Vance (Course Instructor):** Tuesdays 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM and Thursdays 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM in Science Hall, Office 418. Virtual meetings available upon prior email request.
- **Marcus Brody (Head Teaching Assistant):** Mondays and Wednesdays 3:00 PM – 5:00 PM in Turing Lab Room 108. Focuses on programming assignments, environment setup, and debugging.
- **Priya Sharma (Graduate Teaching Assistant):** Fridays 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM in Science Hall Room 310. Specializes in mathematics, embeddings, and vector database concepts.

## 4. Computing Resources and Lab Access
- **Turing Computing Labs:** Open 24/7 for students registered in computing and data science courses. Card-swipe access is required between 8:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
- **High-Performance Compute Cluster (HPC):** Students enrolled in advanced AI courses (such as CS 401) receive GPU allocations (NVIDIA L40S nodes) for training and evaluation. Access requests are handled by submitting a ticket to hpc-support@campus.edu.
- **Student Cloud Credits:** Every CS 401 student receives a $50 Google Cloud & Gemini API voucher credited at the start of the semester. Unused credits expire on May 31, 2027.

## 5. Health, Wellness, and Disability Accommodations
- **Campus Counseling Services:** Located in Health Center, 2nd Floor. Confidential mental health counseling is provided free to all enrolled students. Crisis hotline: (555) 019-4357 (available 24/7).
- **Accessibility & Disability Services (ADS):** Students requiring academic accommodations (such as extended testing time, distraction-reduced testing environments, note-taking support, or accessible digital formats) must register with ADS in Suite 115 of the Student Center. Accommodation letters must be delivered to instructors within the first three weeks of the semester.
